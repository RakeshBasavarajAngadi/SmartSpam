About SmartSpam

SmartSpam is an academic Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning system engineered to detect spam, phishing, and fraudulent communications with high accuracy and low false-positive rates.

1. NLP Text Preprocessing Pipeline

Text normalization converts unstructured natural language into regularized linguistic features:

  • Token Normalization: Replaces URLs with urltoken, emails with emailtoken, phone numbers with phonetoken, and currency with moneytoken.
  • Lowercasing & Tokenization: Uses NLTK's word_tokenize for grammatical token splitting.
  • Negation Preservation: Standard stopword filtering removes "not" and "no", altering sentence meaning. SmartSpam explicitly preserves negations to protect semantic context.
  • Porter Stemming: Reduces inflected words to root stems (e.g., winning → win, prizes → prize).

2. TF-IDF Feature Extraction

Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) quantifies lexical relevance across messages:

  • Term Frequency (TF): Evaluates keyword frequency within each message using sublinear logarithmic scaling ($1 + \log(\text{tf})$).
  • Inverse Document Frequency (IDF): Penalizes ubiquitous terms while amplifying discriminative spam keywords.
  • N-Gram Range: Extracts both unigrams and bigrams (1, 2) to capture critical spam phrases such as "cash prize", "call now", and "claim code".
  • Leakage Prevention: Fitted strictly on the 80% training split.

3. Multinomial Naive Bayes (Baseline)

Multinomial Naive Bayes applies Bayes' Theorem under the conditional independence assumption:

  • Formula: $P(\text{Spam} \mid d) \propto P(\text{Spam}) \prod_{i=1}^n P(w_i \mid \text{Spam})^{f_i}$
  • Strengths: Ultra-fast closed-form training (< 2 ms) with 100% precision at Laplace smoothing ($\alpha = 1.0$).
  • Limitation: Naive feature independence causes multiplied probabilities to polarize heavily toward 0 or 1, making raw probabilities overconfident.

4. Linear Support Vector Machine (Primary)

LinearSVC constructs a maximum-margin hyperplane separating high-dimensional TF-IDF vectors:

  • Maximum Margin: Optimizes separation distance between spam and legitimate message clusters.
  • Benchmark Winner: Achieves 99.03% Accuracy and 96.15% F1-Score with 95.42% spam recall.
  • Platt Scaling Calibration: Employs CalibratedClassifierCV using 5-fold cross-validation on training data to provide genuine calibrated confidence probabilities.
